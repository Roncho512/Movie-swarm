# Camera package: Sergio Leone spaghetti western

Techniscope 35mm (2-perf, 2.39:1). Leone-style sun-blasted grainy spaghetti western, pushed harder. Extreme wide vistas cut against tight eye close-ups. Long tense holds, then sharp cuts. Mostly a still camera, with slow push-ins. Harsh sun. Dusty palette.

## Lenses

- extreme wide (wide spherical): Extreme wide vistas. The land is larger than the rider.
- tight close (long): Tight close-ups on the eyes.

## Framing

- Extreme wide vistas cut against tight eye close-ups.
- Long holds, then sharp cuts. Mostly a still camera, with slow push-ins.

## Lighting

- Harsh sun. Dust in the air. The day moves from amber to bronze to rose to blue dusk.

## Palette

amber, ochre, bronze, rose, cool dusk blue, dust
