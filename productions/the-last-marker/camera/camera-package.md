# Camera package: Sergio Leone spaghetti western

Locked 2.39:1 widescreen. Extreme wide vistas cut against tight eye close-ups. Long tense holds, then sharp cuts. Mostly a still camera, with slow push-ins. Warm sun-bleached 1960s film look, with grain. Harsh sun. Dusty palette.

## Lenses

- extreme wide (wide anamorphic): Extreme wide vistas. The land is larger than the rider.
- tight close (long): Tight close-ups on the eyes.

## Framing

- Extreme wide vistas cut against tight eye close-ups.
- Long holds, then sharp cuts. Mostly a still camera, with slow push-ins.

## Lighting

- Harsh sun. Dust in the air. The day moves from amber to bronze to rose to blue dusk.

## Palette

amber, ochre, bronze, rose, cool dusk blue, dust
