# Storyboard

The look is stated once. Each shot below is one beat. It lists only its own frame.

## Look: Sergio Leone spaghetti western

Locked 2.39:1 widescreen. Extreme wide vistas cut against tight eye close-ups. Long tense holds, then sharp cuts. Mostly a still camera, with slow push-ins. Warm sun-bleached 1960s film look, with grain. Harsh sun. Dusty palette.

Lenses: extreme wide (wide anamorphic): Extreme wide vistas. The land is larger than the rider.; tight close (long): Tight close-ups on the eyes.

Framing: Extreme wide vistas cut against tight eye close-ups. Long holds, then sharp cuts. Mostly a still camera, with slow push-ins.

Lighting: Harsh sun. Dust in the air. The day moves from amber to bronze to rose to blue dusk.

Palette: amber, ochre, bronze, rose, cool dusk blue, dust

Images: not-run. Animatic: 180.447 seconds.
