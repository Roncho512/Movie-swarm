## Logline

A solo motorcycle racer crosses the Baja California desert in 1967. His 1965 Triumph is failing. He is lost. He finds a trail marker left by a rider who came before him. The marker points two ways. He has to choose: water, or the course. Three minutes. Almost no dialogue.

## Theme and emotional intent

Theme: Arrive first. Winning can wait.

Emotional intent: The audience should feel how small the rider is, then the shock of the engine dying, then the quiet relief of a choice to live.

## Tone and genre

Tone: Sparse, tense, and sun-bleached. A Sergio Leone spaghetti western played as a quiet desert survival story.

Genre: western

## Characters

### Miguel Vega

Role: protagonist

Look: 30. Mexican. Lean. Sun-darkened skin, deep lines around the eyes from years of squinting into wind and light. Short dark hair. Strong jaw. The wear is from sun and wind, not from age.

Wardrobe: He wears a white cotton shirt, a sun-bleached brown leather vest, khaki pants, and boots that have been resoled twice. Goggles sit on his forehead when he is not riding. No helmet.

Arc: He wants to finish the race, but in the end water (life) is more important. (He chooses life over winning.)

Continuity: Breitling Top Time: Black reverse panda dial, stainless case, worn brown leather rally strap. He won it in a card game from a crop duster pilot in Mexicali. Locked on the left wrist.

## Scenes

### EXT. BAJA CALIFORNIA - OPEN DESERT - LATE AFTERNOON

EXTREME WIDE. The Baja desert. Flat hardpan stretching to a mountain range on the horizon. Cardon cacti scattered across the landscape like sentries. The sky is enormous. The sun is low in the west, turning everything amber. Hold this frame for four seconds. Empty. Still. Then: A dust trail appears at the far left of the frame. Moving right. Tiny. A white line drawn across the brown landscape. It takes three full seconds for the source to become visible: a motorcycle. A man on a machine, crossing the desert alone. ---

Emotional purpose: The desert is in charge.

### EXT. BAJA CALIFORNIA - ROCKY WASH - MOMENTS LATER

The terrain has changed. The flat hardpan gives way to a rocky wash. Larger stones. Uneven footing. The Triumph picks its way through, Vega standing on the pegs, letting the bike move underneath him. The engine coughs. A hard, wet sound. Vega blips the throttle. The engine catches. Coughs again. He pulls in the clutch. Coasts. Tries the throttle again. The engine sputters, fires unevenly for three seconds, and dies. The Triumph rolls to a stop. Vega puts both boots on the ground. Sits on the bike for a moment. Still. Processing. Then he swings his leg off and lowers the kickstand onto a flat rock. He kneels beside the engine. Pulls off his gloves and lays them on the tank. His bare hands move over the motor with the familiarity of a doctor examining a patient. He checks the fuel petcock. Open. He unscrews the float bowl drain on the carburetor. Emotional purpose: The light carries the feeling. The amber is deepening. Sun dropping fast. Shadows are longer. The warmth is leaving the air. The transition from afternoon to pre-dusk. The light is beautiful and it is running out.

Coverage: Coverage named in the screenplay: CLOSE, WIDE SHOT.

### EXT. BAJA CALIFORNIA - ROCKY WASH - MOMENTS LATER

Vega walks. He has left the bike. He carries nothing. His boots crunch on the rocky ground. He is walking in the direction he was riding, following the faint tire tracks of riders who came through here before him. Looking for something. Any sign of the course. Any marker. DETAIL SHOT: The last drop falls from an empty canteen. Nothing follows it. DETAIL SHOT: His boots on the ground. Worn leather. The sole of the left boot is separating at the toe. Each step kicks up a small cloud of dust that the low sun turns pink. He crests a low rise and stops. WIDE SHOT: Buzzards circle in the distant sky. EXTREME CLOSE. A buzzard's face and beak fill the frame. A hard cut lands on a tight close-up of his eyes. VEGA'S POV: Ahead, fifty meters, a pile of stacked rocks. A cairn. The tin arrow points toward the canyon. Beside it, the word AGUA. The empty canteen is why that word matters.

Emotional purpose: Decision is a physical thing.

Point of view: The rider, for the shot marked POV. The rest of the scene is observed.

### EXT. BAJA CALIFORNIA - ROCKY WASH - CONTINUOUS

Vega walks back to the Triumph. He kneels beside the engine one more time. Pulls the fouled plug. Holds it up. Studies it. Then he does something unexpected. He places the plug on a flat rock. Picks up a smaller stone. Carefully, precisely, he taps the cracked porcelain insulator with the stone. Once. Twice. On the third tap, the loose fragment of cracked porcelain falls away, exposing clean insulator underneath. Not repaired. But the short circuit caused by the crack is eliminated. The plug might fire now. Once. Maybe twice. Maybe long enough. He threads the plug back into the cylinder head. Finger-tight. Reconnects the wire. Swings his leg over the bike. Stands on the left peg. Reaches down with his right foot and finds the kick starter. DETAIL SHOT: His boot on the kick lever. The worn sole. The exposed stitching at the toe. The lever is steel, polished smooth by a thousand kicks…

Emotional purpose: A man who chose to live over a man who chose to win.

Withheld: Withhold whether the canyon really has water.

## Sound and music

The Triumph's single-cylinder engine is the lead sound. When it dies, the desert goes quiet: wind, sand, and cooling metal. One Spanish line, almost lost under the engine. A score in the spirit of Ennio Morricone. Do not copy any melody.

## Color and light

**Scene 1:** Full amber. High saturation. Late afternoon at its warmest. **Scene 2:** Amber cooling to bronze. Shadows lengthening. Warmth starting to drain. **Scene 3:** Bronze to rose. Purple shadows. Sun on the western ridge. Transitional. **Scene 4:** Last horizontal gold. Side-lit. Maximum contrast. Then rapid fade to cool blue dusk. The color arc tells time without a clock. The audience should feel the sun setting.

## Pacing and runtime

Scene 1 is fluid. Longer shots. The camera follows and observes. The pace says: this has been going on for a while. Scene 2 slows down. The engine death creates a hard stop. The pace decelerates with the bike. Scene 3 is the slowest. Walking pace. Contemplation. The cairn discovery is a beat of stillness. Scene 4 accelerates. The kicks are percussive. Quick cuts between boot and face. When the engine catches, the pace lifts. The final wide shot holds long and lets the tension release. The overall shape is: cruising, decelerating, still, erupting, releasing. A heartbeat that stops and restarts.
