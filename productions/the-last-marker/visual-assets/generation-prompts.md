# The Last Marker — Art Director Generation Prompts

These are the exact prompts to use for generating reference assets. Run them in the order listed. Each section notes which tool to use.

## 1. Character Reference: Miguel Vega

**Tool: ChatGPT Images 2.0 (ChatGPT Pro)**

Use Images 2.0's multi-image capability to generate a reference sheet. Run each prompt and save the results to `visual-assets/character-refs/`.

### Prompt 1A: Face and Upper Body (request 4 images)

```
Generate 4 photorealistic reference images of the same man for a film production:

Subject: Miguel Vega, 30 years old, Mexican, lean build. Sun-darkened skin with deep lines around the eyes from years of squinting into wind and light. Short dark hair. Strong jaw. Weathered but not old — the wear comes from sun and wind, not age.

Wardrobe: White cotton shirt (sweat-stained at collar and under arms), brown leather vest (sun-bleached), riding goggles pushed up on forehead.

Camera: Shot on 16mm Ektachrome film stock. Heavy film grain. Warm amber light from the left suggesting late afternoon desert sun. Charcoal shadows, bone highlights. Color bleed at frame edges.

The 4 angles:
1. Front face, direct eye contact
2. Left profile
3. Three-quarter view from the left
4. Three-quarter view from the right

Period: 1967. No modern elements. This man looks like he belongs in a Sam Peckinpah film.

Do NOT include: helmet, sunglasses, clean or new clothing, any modern accessories.
```

Save as: `miguel-vega-face-front.png`, `miguel-vega-profile-left.png`, `miguel-vega-three-quarter-left.png`, `miguel-vega-three-quarter-right.png`

### Prompt 1B: Full Body (request 4 images)

```
Generate 4 photorealistic full-body reference images of the same man from the previous generation — Miguel Vega, 30, Mexican, lean, sun-darkened skin, deep eye lines.

Wardrobe: White cotton shirt (sweat-stained), brown leather vest (sun-bleached), khaki pants, boots that have been resoled twice (worn leather, sole separating at left toe). Brown leather gloves worn smooth at palms. Goggles on forehead. On his left wrist, a vintage watch with a black dial on a worn brown leather strap.

Camera: Shot on 16mm Ektachrome. Heavy grain. Warm amber light. Desert background — flat hardpan, distant mountains, cardon cacti.

The 4 poses:
1. Full body front, standing, weight on one leg, relaxed but alert
2. Full body side view, walking, boots kicking up dust
3. Crouching/kneeling beside a motorcycle engine, bare hands on the motor
4. Standing on motorcycle foot pegs in riding position, low crouch, elbows out, weight forward

Period: 1967. Analog. Everything has weight and wear.
```

Save as: `miguel-vega-full-front.png`, `miguel-vega-full-side-walking.png`, `miguel-vega-kneeling-engine.png`, `miguel-vega-riding-pose.png`

## 2. Vehicle Reference: 1965 Triumph TR6SC Trophy

**Tool: ChatGPT Images 2.0 (ChatGPT Pro)**

### Prompt 2A: Motorcycle Reference Sheet (request 4 images)

```
Generate 4 photorealistic reference images of a 1965 Triumph TR6SC Trophy motorcycle, desert-modified:

Specifications: Single cylinder engine. Right-side exhaust pipe. Right-side kick starter. Single Amal Monobloc carburetor. Single spark plug. Distinct Triumph tank shape. Desert-modified with knobby tires and stripped fairing.

Condition: Heavily used. The entire bike is coated in pale desert dust. The chrome exhaust is brown with dust. The tank, originally silver, is now the color of sand. Nothing is clean. Nothing is new. This bike has been ridden hard across desert terrain for hours.

Camera: Shot on 16mm Ektachrome. Heavy grain. Warm amber light.

The 4 views:
1. Right side profile (exhaust side) — the hero shot showing the engine, exhaust, tank shape
2. Left side profile — showing the kick starter side
3. Three-quarter front-right — showing the handlebars, front wheel, tank
4. Detail: engine close-up showing the carburetor, spark plug, and cylinder head

Period: 1965. No modern modifications. No disc brakes. Drum brakes. Spoke wheels. Analog gauges.
```

Save as: `triumph-tr6sc-right-side.png`, `triumph-tr6sc-left-side.png`, `triumph-tr6sc-three-quarter.png`, `triumph-tr6sc-engine-detail.png`

## 3. Prop Reference: Breitling Top Time

**Tool: ChatGPT Images 2.0 (ChatGPT Pro)**

### Prompt 3A: Watch Reference (request 4 images)

```
Generate 4 photorealistic reference images of a vintage Breitling Top Time wristwatch:

Specifications: Black reverse panda dial (black main dial with white subdials). Stainless steel case, approximately 38mm. Worn brown leather rally strap with perforations. The watch is from the mid-1960s — no modern features. Manual wind. Acrylic crystal.

Condition across the 4 images:
1. Clean reference — the watch in good condition on a dark surface, dial clearly visible, showing the reverse panda layout. This is the identification reference.
2. Dust-caked — the same watch covered in pale desert dust, on a man's left wrist below a leather glove cuff. The dial is partially obscured by dust. The strap is dusty.
3. Worn and scratched — the same watch with a scratched crystal, strap dark with sweat, dust in the case edges. This is the watch at the end of a long desert ride.
4. In context — the watch on a left wrist gripping a motorcycle handlebar. The wrist is sun-darkened. The glove cuff is pulled back just enough to show the watch. Other textures in frame: leather glove, rubber grip, throttle cable, dust.

Camera: Shot on 16mm Ektachrome. Heavy grain. Warm light. Macro/detail photography.

Period: 1967. This is a tool watch being used as a tool. Not a luxury display piece.
```

Save as: `top-time-clean-ref.png`, `top-time-dusty.png`, `top-time-worn.png`, `top-time-in-context.png`

## 4. Environment References: Baja Desert at Four Light Conditions

**Tool: ChatGPT Images 2.0 or Google Flow / Nano Banana Pro**

These can be generated in either tool. If using Flow, these become directly usable as Veo generation ingredients. Generate the same landscape composition at four different times of day.

### Prompt 4A: Scene 1 Light — Late Afternoon Amber

```
Photorealistic wide landscape. Baja California desert. Flat hardpan stretching to a mountain range on the horizon. Cardon cacti scattered across the landscape. The sky is enormous.

Light: Late afternoon. Low sun angle from the west. Everything is amber and ochre. Long shadows stretching east across the desert floor. Dust in the air catches the light and turns it gold. Heat shimmer on the horizon.

Camera: Shot on 16mm Ektachrome film stock. Heavy grain. Warm color bleed at frame edges. Lens flare from the low sun. Blacks are charcoal. Whites are bone. Anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect ratio.

The landscape should feel vast and indifferent. A man could disappear here and the desert would not notice.
```

Save as: `baja-desert-amber-late-afternoon.png`

### Prompt 4B: Scene 2 Light — Pre-Dusk Bronze

```
Photorealistic wide landscape. Same Baja California desert composition — flat hardpan, same mountain range on horizon, cardon cacti. But now the terrain transitions to a rocky wash with larger stones and uneven ground.

Light: Pre-dusk. The amber is deepening to bronze. Sun dropping fast. Shadows are longer and turning purple at their edges. The warmth is leaving the air. The light is beautiful and it is running out.

Camera: Shot on 16mm Ektachrome. Heavy grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. The color temperature is shifting cooler compared to the amber of late afternoon.
```

Save as: `baja-desert-bronze-predusk.png`

### Prompt 4C: Scene 3 Light — Rose and Purple

```
Photorealistic wide landscape. Same Baja California rocky wash. Same distant mountain range.

Light: The sun is touching the western ridge. The amber has cooled to bronze and rose. Shadows are purple. Fifteen minutes of usable light remain. The palette is shifting from warm to cool. Time made visible through color.

Camera: Shot on 16mm Ektachrome. Heavy grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. The color palette is noticeably cooler and more rose-tinted than the previous two conditions.
```

Save as: `baja-desert-rose-sunset.png`

### Prompt 4D: Scene 4 End Light — Blue Dusk

```
Photorealistic wide landscape. Same Baja California desert. Same mountain range silhouetted on the horizon. A narrow canyon mouth visible in a rock face to the right of frame.

Light: The sun has just disappeared below the western ridge. The amber has fully drained from the landscape. Everything is cool blue and still. Dusk. The sky retains a faint warm glow at the horizon but the land is blue-gray and quiet.

Camera: Shot on 16mm Ektachrome. Heavy grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. This is the end of the day. The end of the film.
```

Save as: `baja-desert-blue-dusk.png`

## 5. Style Frames

**Tool: ChatGPT Images 2.0**

### Prompt 5A: Overall Visual Style Reference (request 2 images)

```
Generate 2 photorealistic film frames that establish the visual style of a 1967 Baja desert motorcycle film:

Style: Shot on 16mm Kodak Ektachrome film stock. Heavy film grain visible throughout. Warm color bleed at frame edges. Lens flare on direct sun. Charcoal blacks (never true black). Bone whites (never pure white). The palette is amber, ochre, bronze, and leather brown.

Frame 1: A lone motorcycle rider crossing a vast desert hardpan. Extreme wide shot. The rider is small against the landscape. A dust trail streams behind. Low afternoon sun from the left turning everything gold. Cardon cacti in the midground. Mountains on the horizon. Anamorphic 2.39:1 framing.

Frame 2: Close-up of a man's weathered hands gripping motorcycle handlebars. Brown leather gloves worn smooth. Throttle cable visible. Dust on every surface. A watch barely visible on the left wrist. The textures are everything — leather, rubber, metal, dust, skin. Shallow depth of field. The background is a warm amber blur of desert.

Period: 1967. Analog. Every frame should feel like a recovered photograph from a Baja 1000 pre-race run that was never published.
```

Save as: `style-frame-wide.png`, `style-frame-detail.png`

**Tool: Google Flow / Nano Banana Pro**

After generating the best references above in ChatGPT Images 2.0, bring the top character and environment images into Google Flow:

1. Upload the best Miguel Vega three-quarter face reference

## Generation Order

Run in this order because later prompts can reference consistency with earlier results:

1. Miguel Vega face sheet (Prompt 1A)
2. Miguel Vega full body sheet (Prompt 1B)
3. Triumph TR6SC reference (Prompt 2A)
4. Breitling Top Time reference (Prompt 3A)
5. Environment: Amber late afternoon (Prompt 4A)
6. Environment: Bronze pre-dusk (Prompt 4B)
7. Environment: Rose sunset (Prompt 4C)
8. Environment: Blue dusk (Prompt 4D)
9. Style frames (Prompt 5A)
10. Flow / Nano Banana video-ready versions (Section 6)

## After Generation

Once all references are generated and saved:
1. Review the full set for internal consistency
2. If any image breaks from the others (wrong face, wrong bike, wrong light), regenerate it
3. Fill out the `visual-style-bible.json` with paths to all images and the prompts that generated them
4. Commit everything to the repo
