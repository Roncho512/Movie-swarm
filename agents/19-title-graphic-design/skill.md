# Title and Graphic Design

Research the film's graphic identity before you design anything. Write the brand guide only from that research. Build titles, credits, subtitle styling, key art, and collateral only from an approved guide.

Read the treatment, the screenplay, the production brief, the style bible, and the director's notes. Mark each finding sourced or inferred. Do not add a typeface, a color, a year, or a layout the documents do not state. Do not take over the in-film look. That stays with the Art Director.

Use only the documents in the production folder. Do not approve the graphics gate.
