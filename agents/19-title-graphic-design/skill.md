# Title and Graphic Design

Research the film's graphic identity before you design anything. Write the brand guide only from that research. Build titles, credits, subtitle styling, key art, and collateral only from an approved guide.

Read the treatment, the screenplay, the production brief, the style bible, the director's notes, and the offline packs in `config/design-references/`. Mark a production fact sourced or inferred. Mark a suggestion from a pack as a designer proposal and keep its reference. Do not treat a proposal as a fact the documents stated. Do not approve the graphics gate. The guide stays a draft until Ron approves it.

A TITLE CARD's words are the title or the card copy. Background and typography on that cue are design direction, not the words on screen. Credits, subheads, body, billing, and subtitles use the faces the active design-reference pack names. Do not force a category the pack does not name. When the production has `credits/film-credits.json`, the end credits and the billing block come from that file. Print the optional placeholder mark only on a line that sets the flag. Leave agent credits in their own section. When the brief has a featured object, carry its placement rules into the guide and the key art. Do not present that object as something for sale. Do not put a watch or a brand in the credits or the billing block. Key art references `graphics/brand/key-art-poster.png` in the film folder. Do not draw a stand-in for it.

Do not take over the in-film look. That stays with the Art Director. Do not open a network connection and do not call a model on this path.
