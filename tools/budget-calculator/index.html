Film budget

One budget. One screen. The trailer is a line on this film, not a second calculator. Come in under the number, or a little over it with Ron's approval.

Shares are percentages of the film budget and add up to 100. When the trailer is on, Marketing and Distribution takes its share and the other lines scale down so the total still matches. Contingency is held back inside the budget. Ten percent is the proposed default for each. A film with no file keeps the $500 split.
